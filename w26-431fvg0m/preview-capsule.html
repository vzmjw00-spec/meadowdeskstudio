        Care

        Keep the expensive look until spring

        Cashmere and merino

        Hand wash cold or use the wool cycle, dry flat, fold, and never hang. A fabric comb removes pills.

        Silk (washable)

        Wash cold on gentle, inside out, and hang to dry. Check the label first.

        Wool coat

        Brush after wearing, air it out, and dry clean once a season.

        Leather and suede

        Apply suede brush and water-repellent spray before the first snow; wipe leather clean and use leather cream.

        Down puffer

        Wash rarely; tumble dry low with dryer balls so the down doesn't clump.

        Always follow the care label on your piece.
